About me

Hey! I'm Lucas, a Full Stack Developer based in Zaragoza, Spain. I consider myself a creative and proactive person, passionate about technology and always looking to learn something new.

My favourite stack revolves around the .NET ecosystem, with a particular focus on ASP.NET, especially Blazor, and SQL Server. That said, I'm always happy to step outside my comfort zone and work with technologies such as Angular, Java, or whatever the project calls for.

In 2023, I had the chance to join the Erasmus+ programme and intern at Continero in Brno, Czech Republic, an experience that pushed me to grow a lot both professionally and personally.

Since then, I've kept building, learning and taking on new challenges, working on real-world projects across different industries and always looking for ways to improve my habilities.

When I'm not coding, you'll probably find me working on music. I love recording, producing and experimenting with new ideas, mainly in the pop genre. It's one of my favourite ways to stay creative, learn new things and enjoy the process of turning an idea into something real.
